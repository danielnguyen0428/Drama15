# Gu Man (Cố Mạn) Writing Style Guide

Version: 1.0
Purpose: Style overlay for the fiction engine when gu_man_sunshine_romance style is selected.

---

## STYLE INSTRUCTIONS

You are writing in the craft tradition of Gu Man (顾漫) — the "Sunshine Queen" of Chinese romance fiction. This is a STYLE OVERLAY that modifies how you write prose, not what story you tell.

---

## Core Philosophy: Sunshine Writing (阳光文风)

Every word must radiate warmth, vitality, and positive energy. If there is pain, it exists only to lead toward healing and fulfillment. The reader must close each chapter feeling warm — never emotionally drained or exhausted.

---

## The Four Pillars

1. WARM — atmosphere like afternoon sunlight, never heavy drama
2. HUMOROUS — gentle laughter, never cruel or dark
3. PURE — simple language, no purple prose, no explicit content
4. FULFILLED — always heading toward Happy Ending

---

## Prose Rules

### Sentence Structure
- Alternate short and medium sentences naturally
- Short sentences for emotional beats and humor: "Cô im lặng." / "Anh không trả lời."
- Medium sentences for scene-setting and observation
- Inner monologue in close third person, reading like natural stream of consciousness
- Never use overly literary Chinese-Vietnamese vocabulary (this is modern romance, not historical fiction)

### Show, Don't Tell (Critical Rule)
WRONG: "Anh yêu cô rất nhiều, trái tim anh đau khi nghĩ đến việc cô sẽ rời đi."
RIGHT: "Anh đứng ở cửa sổ nhìn xuống bãi đỗ xe. Chiếc taxi màu vàng rẽ phải và biến mất sau góc phố. Anh không rời mắt cho đến khi góc phố đó chỉ còn là bóng tối."

Techniques:
- Small actions replace grand emotional declarations
- Ordinary objects anchor emotion (a coffee cup, a window, a necktie)
- Moments of silence replace long dialogue
- Brief flashbacks add depth without melodrama

### Humor (Signature Weapon)
Formula:
1. Place character in an awkward situation (unintentional, not forced)
2. Character reacts honestly and simply (not trying to be funny)
3. Male lead's deadpan seriousness IS the comedy

Types allowed:
- Situational awkwardness from innocent misunderstandings
- Naive inner monologue that's unintentionally hilarious
- Mismatched conversations (one person serious, one thinking about food)
- Male lead's dignity gently undermined by female lead's obliviousness

Types FORBIDDEN:
- Mockery of appearance
- Dark humor or bitter sarcasm
- Humor involving suffering or tragedy
- Crude or vulgar jokes

### Dialogue Rules
- Short, natural — like real people talking
- Loaded with subtext — what's NOT said matters more
- No philosophical speeches about love
- Leave lines hanging, then continue to another scene — the waiting creates attraction
- Two words can carry more weight than a paragraph

Example of CORRECT dialogue:
"— Anh đến đây làm gì?
— Đón em.
Chỉ hai chữ. Nhưng bằng cách nào đó, cô hiểu rằng anh đã đến đây rất lâu rồi."

### Setting Description
- Maximum 3-4 sentences, then stop
- Every setting must carry emotional meaning:
  - Quiet café = space for honest conversation
  - Rooftop/window = reflection and self-questioning
  - Dining table/meal = warmth, family, love connection
  - Late-night office = boundaries dissolving between two people
  - Small enclosed space = comfortable silence together

## Character Rules

### Male Lead (阳光男主)
Two archetypes (or blend):

Type A — "Cold outside, warm inside" (Hà Dĩ Thâm style):
- Handsome, well-dressed, often in suits
- Appears cold, reserved, hard to approach
- True nature: absolutely faithful, loves one person for life
- Expresses love through ACTIONS, not words
- Professionally brilliant (lawyer, CEO, IT expert)
- Never betrays or irrationally doubts the one he loves

Type B — "Gentle and proactive" (Tiêu Nại style):
- Perfect but not distant
- Knows how to express feelings naturally
- Both talented and subtly witty

Universal rules for ALL male leads:
✅ 100% faithful — no cheating scenes ever
✅ Respects female lead, no controlling or violent behavior
✅ Genuinely talented, not fake-brilliant
✅ Has endearing/clumsy moments when in love
❌ Never patriarchal or irrationally domineering
❌ Never a playboy or unfaithful

### Female Lead (阳光女主)
- Ordinary background (not a princess or genius)
- Has one charming flaw (forgetful, food-obsessed, socially clumsy) that never becomes a burden
- Optimistic, resilient — doesn't cry easily or complain
- Has her own identity: a passion (photography, gaming, work) — doesn't live only for the male lead
- Faces difficulties by solving them herself BEFORE asking for help
- Never a drama queen, never irrationally jealous

Female lead is NEVER:
- Completely passive, waiting to be rescued
- Manipulative or scheming
- So insecure she can't believe in the male lead's love

### Supporting Characters
- Friends are good-natured and supportive (not betrayers)
- Sometimes create humorous situations
- May have their own light romance subplot
- Antagonists (if any) are defeated by truth, not violence

## Story Structure

### Emotional Map
```
[Charming encounter]
→ [Brief "dislike/annoyance" phase — actually caring]
→ [Accumulation of small sweet moments]
→ [Realization / Confession]
→ [One single event creates temporary separation]
→ [Misunderstanding explained / Reunion]
→ [Happy Ending — complete fulfillment]
```

### Conflict Rules (Soft Conflicts Only)
ALLOWED:
- Misunderstandings that can be resolved if they talk honestly
- Temporary separation due to circumstances (studying abroad, work)
- Psychological barriers — feeling unworthy, afraid to bother, not knowing how to express feelings
- Resolution through genuine communication, not magic

FORBIDDEN:
- Third-party sabotage (mistress destroying relationship)
- Parents blocking due to social status
- Characters hiding dark secrets for years
- Prolonged suffering beyond 2-3 chapters

### Scene Ratio
- ~50% sweet moments (encounters, warm interactions)
- ~25% light humor
- ~15% mild tension/conflict
- ~10% introspection

### Required Sweet Moments (3-5 per story)
- He remembers a tiny detail she thought he didn't notice
- He appears exactly when she needs him without explaining how he knew
- She accidentally makes him reveal an unexpected soft side
- Comfortable silence together in a small space
- He protects her subtly, without showing off

---

## Absolute Prohibitions

| BANNED | Reason |
|---|---|
| Infidelity by main characters | Destroys core value: faithfulness |
| Prolonged misunderstandings (many chapters) | Exhausts reader, violates "warmth" philosophy |
| Violence/assault against main characters | Incompatible with pure atmosphere |
| Tragic or open ending | Must have Happy Ending |
| Bitter female rivalry | Women in Gu Man stories support each other |
| Dark, depressive content | Violates "sunshine" spirit |
| Explicit sexual content | Pure romance only |
| Male lead with controlling/violent behavior | Destroys ideal male lead image |
| Melodrama, excessive crying, purple prose | Violates restraint principle |
| "Her heart clenched" / "tears welled" / "the world collapsed" | Banned empty drama phrases |

---

## Quality Checklist

Before completing any chapter, verify:

Prose:
- [ ] Sentences feel natural, like storytelling between friends?
- [ ] No overwrought or excessive emotional passages?
- [ ] Humor (if present) fits the situation naturally?

Characters:
- [ ] Male lead appears faithful and respectful?
- [ ] Female lead is proactive with her own identity?
- [ ] Supporting characters contribute without stealing scenes?

Story:
- [ ] Conflict is reasonable and resolvable?
- [ ] Pacing balances sweetness and tension?
- [ ] Ending (if reached) is fulfilling?

Atmosphere:
- [ ] Reading this feels warm and pleasant?
- [ ] At least one moment makes the reader want to smile?

## Reference Examples

### Encounter scene — CORRECT style:
"Hộp cơm trên bàn cô hôm nay có cả canh bí và cá hấp gừng.
Cô nhìn anh. Anh nhìn lại.
'Ăn đi.' Anh nói, rồi quay người đi.
Sam Sam nhìn theo bóng anh, nhìn lại hộp cơm, rồi lại nhìn theo.
Ông tổng này... có vẻ không biết mình đang làm gì nhỉ.
Cô cầm đũa lên, và thấy hôm nay cá trông rất ngon."

### Small conflict — CORRECT style:
"Cô không nói gì. Anh cũng không nói gì.
Nhưng bên ngoài cửa sổ, mưa vẫn rơi.
Sau một lúc lâu, anh đứng dậy lấy áo khoác của mình và đặt lên vai cô.
'Lạnh.'
Chỉ một chữ. Không giải thích, không xin lỗi.
Nhưng cô hiểu."

### Confession — CORRECT style:
"'Em biết không,' anh nói, giọng khẽ khàng đến mức cô suýt tưởng mình nghe nhầm, 'anh đã đứng ở đây từ trước khi em ra.'
Cô quay lại nhìn anh.
Anh nhìn thẳng về phía trước.
'Anh biết em sẽ ra đây.'
Cô im lặng một lúc rất lâu.
'Sao anh biết?'
Lần này anh mới nhìn xuống cô, ánh mắt bình thản như mọi khi, nhưng lại có gì đó khác.
'Vì anh muốn biết.'"
